Overview

Amanda Filipacchi is a French-born novelist and Hamilton College alumna. She is known for her surrealist fiction, including the novel Nude Men (1993) and Vapor (1995). She attended Hamilton in the mid-1980s.

Relevance to Research

Filipacchi appears in the 1985–86 and 1986–87 course catalogs as a student enrolled at Hamilton, listed under France as her home country. The 1985–86 catalog lists her as “Amanda Filipacchi, France” and the 1986–87 catalog lists her as “Amanda Filipacchi, Bretech, France,” indicating she was enrolled across at least two academic years.

Notes

The catalog entries confirm her enrollment but give no detail beyond her name and home location. No additional context appears in these issues.
