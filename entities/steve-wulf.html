Overview

Steve Wulf is a sports journalist and Hamilton College alumnus, known for his work at Sports Illustrated and ESPN. During his time at Hamilton he was active in student journalism, serving as Subscription Manager for The Spectator.

Relevance to Research

Both the September 20, 1969 Spectator (spec-1969-09-20) and the September 26, 1969 Spectator (spec-1969-09-26) list “Steve Wulf” as Subscription Manager on the Spectator masthead. Both issues carry the same masthead, with Wulf named alongside Donald Edwards (local advertising manager) and Dan Keane (circulation manager). This places him as an active Spectator staff member in fall 1969, consistent with a likely graduation around 1971 or 1972.

Notes

Wulf appears only in the masthead in both issues; no articles bylined to him appear in these two files. The mentions are consistent across both issues, confirming his role.
