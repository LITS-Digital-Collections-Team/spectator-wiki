Overview

James R. Lawrence (Class of 1943) was a Hamilton College student in the early 1940s who served in an athletic management role during his undergraduate years. He appears in the college’s records from the 1942–43 academic year as Varsity Manager of the fencing team, a recognized leadership position in Hamilton’s athletic program at the time.

Relevance to Research
