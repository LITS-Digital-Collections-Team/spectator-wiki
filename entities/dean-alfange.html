Dean Alfange

Overview

Dean Alfange (1899–1989) was an American lawyer, politician, and liberal activist who co-founded the Liberal Party of New York in 1944 and ran as the Liberal Party’s candidate for Governor of New York in 1942. He was a Hamilton College alumnus who graduated in the early 1920s. His 1942 gubernatorial campaign — which drew enough votes to defeat Thomas Dewey’s margin against Herbert Lehman — established him as a significant force in New York liberal politics and led directly to the founding of the Liberal Party as a permanent institution.
