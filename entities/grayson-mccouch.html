Overview

Donald Grayson McCouch was a member of Hamilton College’s class of 1965 (appearing in the 1964–65 catalog). He later became known as the actor Grayson McCouch, appearing in daytime television dramas and other roles.

Relevance to Research

McCouch appears in the 1964–65 Hamilton course catalog in a roster of students, listed under his full name “Donald Grayson McCouch.” He also appears in the catalog’s class listing alongside other members of his cohort.

Notes

The catalog lists him by his full legal name “Donald Grayson McCouch.” He is professionally known as Grayson McCouch. The catalog entry is an enrollment listing with no additional biographical detail.
