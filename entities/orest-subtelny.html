Orest Subtelny

Overview

Orest Subtelny (1941–2016) was a Ukrainian-Canadian historian best known as the author of Ukraine: A History (1988), one of the most widely read histories of Ukraine in the English language. He was born in Cracow, Poland, to Ukrainian parents who emigrated to the United States after World War II. He served as Assistant Professor of History at Hamilton College from 1975 to approximately 1984, before moving to York University in Toronto.

Relevance to Research

Subtelny appears consistently across the Hamilton corpus from 1975 through the 1983–84 academic year — spanning nine consecutive course catalogs and four issues of the Hamilton Spectator. Coverage is both administrative (faculty listings, tenure review) and journalistic (feature interview, Soviet study-trip reports, conference notices). The Spectator profile from September 1975 is especially rich, establishing his background and his assessment of Hamilton compared to Harvard. All mentions are contemporary, not retrospective.
