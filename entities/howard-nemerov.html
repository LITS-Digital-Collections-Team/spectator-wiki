Howard Nemerov

Overview

Howard Nemerov (Hamilton College Class of 1941) was a major American poet who later became U.S. Poet Laureate (1988–1990) and won both the National Book Award and the Pulitzer Prize. He returned to Hamilton as an English faculty member in the mid-1940s, making him both an alumnus and a member of the teaching staff during the immediate post-war years. His wife, Margaret “Peggy” Nemerov, also participated in Hamilton campus life during his faculty tenure.

Relevance to Research

The Hamiltonews archive documents Nemerov’s presence at Hamilton during the 1944–1947 period, when he is listed as an English faculty member. The most specific reference to the Nemerov family in the archive appears in the May 22, 1947 issue, which names “Mrs. Peggy Nemerov” as a cast member playing Miranda in the Charlatans’ production of Shakespeare’s The Tempest, performed at Alumni Gymnasium. The source notes she was English-born and the only woman in the cast. Howard Nemerov himself is identified in the archive as an English faculty member during this period.
