A. Ross Eckler

Overview

Albert Ross Eckler (1901–1991) was a statistician and federal administrator who served as Deputy Director (1949) and Acting Director (1965–1969) of the U.S. Bureau of the Census. A Hamilton alumnus of the Class of 1922, he was from Jordanville, N.Y., and was a Oren Root Mathematical Scholar during his time at Hamilton. He earned his M.A. and Ph.D. at Harvard, where he taught public utility economics before entering federal government service in 1935. He received an honorary D.Sc. from Hamilton College in 1966.
