Henry Randall Waite

Overview

Henry Randall Waite (class of 1868) was a Hamilton alumnus who earned a Ph.D. and became a prominent advocate for electoral reform in the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. He served as president of the American Proportional Representation League, working to advance proportional representation as an alternative to winner-take-all electoral systems in the United States. He is listed in corpus catalogs at 120 Broadway, New York City, indicating a legal or professional career based there.

Relevance to Research

Waite appears by full name in ten course catalogs spanning 1894-95 through 1901-02, always in the alumni class secretary or class roll sections as the representative of the class of 1868. He is consistently identified as “Rev. Henry Randall Waite, Ph.D.” with a New York City address. The 1903-04 catalog also notes a donation of 10 volumes to the college library from him. His appearance in the 1868-69 catalog has not been confirmed (no full-name match found there), suggesting that catalog either predates the alumni roll conventions or OCR degradation obscures his listing. All corpus mentions are retrospective alumni records rather than contemporary student coverage.
