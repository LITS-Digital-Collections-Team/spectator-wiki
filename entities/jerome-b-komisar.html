Overview

Jerome B. Komisar was an assistant professor of economics at Hamilton College in the mid-1960s and a Hamilton alumnus. During his time on the faculty he taught in the economics department and was an active commentator on college life and academic culture. He left Hamilton in 1966 to join the faculty at Harpur College (now Binghamton University).

Relevance to Research

The March 1966 Spectator (spec-1966-03-18) quotes Komisar in an article about graduate school pressures on undergraduates, where he cautions students against assuming they must specialize in economics before entering graduate programs. The April 1966 Spectator (spec-1966-04-29) profiles Komisar as one of four faculty members departing Hamilton that spring. He cited salary, inadequate research facilities (specifically the lack of a computer with full-time service and insufficient government periodicals in the library), and a lack of intellectual diversity among faculty as reasons for leaving. He also criticized what he saw as the College’s tendency to cultivate politically disengaged students.
