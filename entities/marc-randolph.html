Marc Randolph

Overview

Marc Randolph (born 1958) is an American entrepreneur best known as the co-founder and first CEO of Netflix, the company he launched with Reed Hastings in 1997 that transformed the global entertainment industry. Before Netflix, Randolph co-founded or held senior positions at several technology and direct-mail companies. He graduated from Hamilton College with the Class of 1980.

Relevance to Research

Randolph’s Hamilton education in the late 1970s — during the early years of coeducation following the Kirkland merger — preceded a career that produced one of the most disruptive companies in entertainment history. As the person who conceived the idea of a mail-order DVD rental service that became Netflix, he is among Hamilton’s most economically influential alumni. His memoir That Will Never Work (2019) recounts the founding of Netflix.
