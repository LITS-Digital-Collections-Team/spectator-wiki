Harry D. Yates

Overview

Harry D. Yates (Class of 1925) was a Hamilton College student from Buffalo, N.Y., who was a prominent figure in campus athletics and student life during the early 1920s. He was a varsity hockey player, class president, and Photographic Editor of the yearbook. Post-graduation, he maintained ties to Hamilton as an alumnus. His later career led to the judiciary in New York State; the corpus does not contain specific post-graduation professional detail beyond his Buffalo address.

Relevance to Research
