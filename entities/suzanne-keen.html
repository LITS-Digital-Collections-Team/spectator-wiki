Overview

Suzanne Keen is an English professor and scholar of narrative theory who served as Vice President for Academic Affairs and Dean of Faculty at Hamilton College beginning in 2018. She is a specialist in Victorian literature and the theory of narrative empathy, having previously held a long-term appointment at Washington and Lee University. At Hamilton she combined administrative leadership with her scholarly identity as a faculty member in the English department.
