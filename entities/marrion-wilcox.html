Overview

Marrion Wilcox was a Hamilton College alumnus of the class of 1880 who pursued a career as an author and editor. He is remembered as a literary figure of the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

Relevance to Research

The December 14, 1920 issue of Hamilton Life includes a brief alumni directory entry listing “1880. Marrion Wilcox, author.” in a list of notable alumni organized by class year. This is the sole mention in the corpus.

Notes

The corpus mention is extremely brief — a three-word identification in an alumni list. The spelling “Marrion” (rather than “Marion”) is consistent with the name as it appears in external sources and should be treated as the correct form.
