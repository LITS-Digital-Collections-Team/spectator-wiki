Frank Anechiarico

Overview

Frank Michael Anechiarico is a political scientist and legal scholar who has spent virtually his entire career at Hamilton College, where he is the Maynard-Knox Professor of Government and Law. He graduated from Hamilton in 1971, returned to join the faculty in 1976 as an Instructor in Government after completing graduate work at Indiana University, and rose through the ranks to full professor and a named chair. His scholarship focuses on public administration, government ethics, and anti-corruption policy.

Relevance to Research

Anechiarico appears in corpus sources spanning more than five decades, first as a student (Spectator, 1970–1971) and then as a long-serving faculty member and program director (course catalogs, 1984–2024). He is one of the most continuously documented individuals in the corpus, mentioned in virtually every catalog from 1984-85 through 2024-25.
