Overview

Keith Falconer Fletcher was a Hamilton College alumnus, graduating in the early 1920s. He appears in the college record as both a student attendee at social events and a listed member of the graduating class.

Relevance to Research

The May 1916 Hamilton Life (hamilton-life-1916-05-16) mentions “Keith Fletcher of Sidney” in a list of guests at what appears to be a college social event, identifying him by his hometown of Sidney, New York. The 1922-23 course catalog (yhm-arc-pub-cat-1922-23) lists “Keith Falconer Fletcher” among the graduating class, confirming his full name and placing his graduation around 1922 or 1923.
