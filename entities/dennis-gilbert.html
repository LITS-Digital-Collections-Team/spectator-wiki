Dennis Gilbert

Overview

Dennis Gilbert is a sociologist and long-serving faculty member at Hamilton College, known for his work on social stratification and the American class system. He first appeared at Hamilton and Kirkland Colleges as a visiting instructor in 1975–76 and was subsequently appointed a full Instructor in Sociology in 1976. He holds an M.A. from the University of Oregon and completed his Ph.D. at Cornell University.

Relevance to Research

Gilbert appears in the corpus beginning with the September 1975 Spectator, which lists him among new Kirkland College faculty as a “visiting instructor of sociology” in the Social Science Division. The September 1976 Spectator notes his conversion to a permanent Instructor in Sociology appointment. By December 1975, Gilbert is referenced as a supervisor for student senior projects in sociology, and his name recurs across more than 50 issues spanning from 1975 onward, tracking his long tenure at Hamilton. His presence in the corpus documents the development of the sociology program at both Hamilton and the merged Hamilton-Kirkland institution.
