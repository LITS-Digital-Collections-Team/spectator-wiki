Barrett Seaman

Overview

Barrett Seaman (born 1943) is an American journalist and author who served as a senior editor and correspondent at Time magazine and is best known in the Hamilton College context for his book Binge: What Your College Student Won’t Tell You (2005), which documented the drinking culture at Hamilton College through a year of immersive reporting. Seaman is a Hamilton alumnus and the book drew significant Spectator coverage. He appears in an estimated 190+ files across the ingested Hamilton corpus.

Relevance to Research

Seaman’s Binge (2005) is one of the most direct external examinations of Hamilton’s campus culture in the corpus period. The book’s investigation of alcohol culture, sexual assault, and student life at a small liberal arts college (set explicitly at Hamilton) generated substantial campus discussion and Spectator coverage in 2005–2006. The book’s findings also intersected with the college’s evolving policies on alcohol and Greek life under President Joan Hinde Stewart.
